

| Requirement ID | Requirement Description | Acceptance Criteria |
|---|---|---|
| NFR-01 | The system shall provide reliable temperature monitoring. | Temperature readings shall be recorded at intervals of no more than 10 seconds. |
| NFR-02 | The system shall respond quickly to critical temperature deviations. | A critical temperature alert shall be generated within 5 seconds of detection. |
| NFR-03 | The system shall maintain reliable communication between the vehicle and control system. | Communication failures shall be detected within 10 seconds. |
| NFR-04 | The system shall protect delivery and vehicle data from unauthorized access. | Only authenticated and authorized users shall access restricted functions. |
| NFR-05 | The system shall maintain a record of important operational events. | System events shall include timestamps and relevant event details. |
