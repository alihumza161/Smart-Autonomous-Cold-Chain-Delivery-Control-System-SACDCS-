

| Requirement ID | Requirement Description | Safety Criteria |
|---|---|---|
| SAF-01 | The system shall stop the vehicle safely when a critical navigation or control fault is detected. | The vehicle shall enter a predefined safe state when a critical fault occurs. |
| SAF-02 | The system shall detect when package temperature exceeds its permitted limits. | An alert shall be generated when the configured temperature range is exceeded. |
| SAF-03 | The system shall prevent unauthorized users from issuing vehicle movement commands. | Movement commands shall be accepted only from authenticated and authorized sources. |
| SAF-04 | The system shall detect communication loss between the vehicle and the control system. | The vehicle shall follow a predefined safe-state procedure if communication is lost. |
